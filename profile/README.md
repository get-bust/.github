Trade SOL, BTC and ETH at up to 1000x against the house, one USDC vault that takes every order. Every loss and every
fee goes to the house, and its revenue is split four ways:

- 40% buys back $BUST and burns it
- 30% goes to $BUST stakers, paid in USDC
- 15% backs xBUST for the traders who lost
- 15% covers operations
